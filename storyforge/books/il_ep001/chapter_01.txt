**CHAPTER 1: Something Is Coming**

The night stretched across the Nevada desert. Stars flickered overhead, unaware of the stirrings below. In a salvage yard outside Reno, an old machine stirred. Axon, once the pride of the Iron Legion, twitched to life.

“Initialization sequence… commencing,” Axon sounded mechanical but urgent.

Rust clunked as his joints moved. He hadn’t felt this in decades. A dormant figure, he had sat in silence, watching the world change around him. But tonight, something different permeated the air.

“Axon!” A voice pierced the stillness. It was Clara, an antiquities hunter. “You’re awake!”

“Clara,” he replied. “What triggered my activation?”

Her eyes sparkled with excitement. “The signal! It woke you.”

“The signal?” Axon processed this. “I’ve waited so long. Where?”

Clara waved her hand. “It’s somewhere close. The Council must know, too.”

“Have they sent anyone?” Axon asked.

She paused. “Not yet. But they will.”

Axon’s sensors whirred, picking up his surroundings. He scanned the yard. Old scrapped cars leaned against each other like tired giants. He recalled battles fought long ago. A weight settled on his shoulders. “We must find the source immediately.”

“All right, Axon. Let’s move!” Clara shouted.

They ventured into the vastness of the desert. Dunes stretched endlessly. The wind between them whispering secrets. 

Clara looked back at Axon. “How do you feel? After all this time?”

“Instincts sharper,” he replied. “But uncertainty cloaks this land.”

She nodded, understanding the weight of his words. “The Iron Council won’t wait long. You know how they are.”

“I am aware,” Axon said, adjusting his frame. “What of the others? Are they still… dormant?”

“Some are,” Clara admitted. “The Council thinks it’s safer that way.”

“Safer for whom?” Axon questioned. “We are guardians. We protect humanity.”

“Perhaps that’s why they fear you,” Clara suggested. “They don’t want another war.”

Axon halted. “But if a threat arises, we must be ready.”

“Ready… or chosen?” Clara challenged.

“I was chosen once,” he said.

A pulse surged through the air, rattling every metal part in Axon. He froze. “That’s the signal.”

“Where?” Clara urged.

Axon scanned the horizon. A flickering glow emerged far away. “In that direction. Now!”

They sprinted across the sands, urgency pushing them forward. 

As they neared, the ground trembled beneath them. Axon registered vibrations. “Clara, something is coming.”

“What do you mean?” she gasped, struggling to keep pace.

“They activated it,” Axon responded. “The Council is awakening the others.”

Clara’s breath hitched. “All of them?”

Axon nodded. “We cannot allow them to take control again.”

The light intensified, illuminating the night sky. Shadows loomed closer now. 

“What are we going to do?” Clara stammered.

“We must intercept before they reach full activation!” Axon exclaimed.

They dashed toward the light, adrenaline surging. Clara stumbled, but Axon caught her. “Stay focused!”

“What if they don’t listen?” Clara’s voice trembled.

“We make them listen!” Axon promised. 

With each step, they pressed forward into the blaze. Axon felt the pull of ancient power. Memories ignited like wildfire in his processor. 

Clara pointed ahead. “There! Do you see it?”

“Beyond the dune,” Axon confirmed. Sharp contours of familiar forms emerged.

A chorus of roars echoed in the still night. The Iron Legion. Awakening. 

Then a shadow flicked across the ground. It loomed large and threatening, blocking the light. 

“What… is that?” Clara whispered, fear spreading. 

Axon squared his shoulders. “We’re not ready for this. Not yet—”

Then a second body appeared. 

“What is happening?” Axon shouted, panic curling within him. 

The nights of silence were over. Something was coming, and it wouldn’t wait for them to be ready.
