### Chapter 8: Stormy Becomes Part of the Team

“Look, Stormy!” Little Zeus shouted, pointing at the sky. “You’re officially one of us now!”

Stormy hovered above the garden, his colors shifting from dull grey to a glimmering silver-blue. “I never thought I’d fit in,” he grumbled, but a hint of warmth appeared in his voice.

Athena adjusted her glasses, raising an eyebrow. “You always belonged here. We just needed to see you for who you are.” 

“Really? You mean that?” Stormy asked uncertainly. 

“Of course! You make the garden grow,” Baby Hercules chimed in. His arms extended as he reached for a puddle.

“Like a watering can!” Athena added, giggling. 

Every Tuesday, Stormy transformed the sky. The farmers looked up, waved, and smiled. Little kids dashed out, splashing in puddles. Hercules jumped from one puddle to the next, giggling as he landed with a big splash. 

“Jump higher, Hercules!” Little Zeus cheered. He pointed to a tiny puddle. “Jump in that one too!”

Hercules grinned and leaped—splash! Water sprayed in all directions. 

“Not too high, buddy!” Athena cautioned. “We don’t want the garden washed away.”

“But I like splashes,” Hercules replied, his cheeks puffed out in delight. 

Stormy saw that joy and felt something shift within him. “You really like this? The rain? The splashes?” he asked. 

“Absolutely,” Little Zeus answered. “You make everything better! It’s our time together.”

“Can I really help just by being here?” Stormy wondered aloud. 

“Yes!” Little Zeus giggled. “You rain because you care. It's comforting!” 

Pride swelled within Stormy, and the thundercloud rumbled pleasantly. “I can do this,” he declared, feeling like a part of the family.

Tuesday rolled around, and Stormy focused. He concentrated on making gentle rainfall. He felt something wonderful bubbling inside. “Here we go!” he announced. 

As drops spilled from him, the garden flourished. Flowers danced, and leaves glistened. Little Zeus and Hercules ran in circles, spinning peacefully under the rain. 

“This is the best day ever!” Hercules yelled, stomping in a puddle. 

“Do it again, Stormy!” Athena urged. 

“More rainfall, please!” Little Zeus begged, eyes sparkling. 

Stormy radiated happiness. “You really love me!” 

“Of course, we love you!” Little Zeus grinned. “We’re a team!”

Every Tuesday became special. But one day, as rain fell, something felt off. Thunder growled, and clouds darkened. Stormy flickered between grey hues. 

“What’s wrong?” Athena asked, noticing the change. 

“I— I feel different today. Like I’m missing something,” Stormy confessed.

Little Zeus touched his heart. “We’re still here with you. Just breathe!”

As Stormy took a deep breath, a sudden storm whipped up beyond him. 

“Uh oh! That’s not from me!” he exclaimed. 

A ripple of panic coursed through the crew. “What’s happening?!” Little Zeus shouted. 

Stormy trembled. “I don’t know! I can’t control it!”

Suddenly, a blinding flash lit the darkening sky. Everyone looked up, eyes wide. The locals below gasped in fear. 

Then a second body appeared. 

The crew froze in shock. 

“Who is that?” Little Zeus whispered, heart racing.
