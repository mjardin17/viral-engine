### Chapter 3: Baby Hercules to the Rescue (Sort Of)

Zeus shifted nervously on the steps. “What do you see, Athena?”

“Look at that cloud,” she replied. Her finger pointed to the ominous sky. “It glows like gold.”

Hercules squinted up. “Do you think it’s the thunderbolt?”

Athena nodded slowly. “It’s possible. But we need to be careful. It might not be friendly.”

Zeus shook his head. “That storm cloud has been growing since daybreak. We can’t ignore it.”

“Let me help! I can throw it!” Hercules exclaimed, his eyes bright with excitement.

“Throw what?” Athena raised an eyebrow.

“The storm cloud! I can lift it!” Hercules flexed his chubby arms, oblivious to the serious situation at hand.

“Athena,” Zeus said, sighing, “maybe we should stick to a plan.”

“A plan is good,” Athena agreed. “What’s your idea, then?”

“Umm…” Hercules furrowed his brow. “We find the thunderbolt first, then we throw the cloud?”

“Sounds logical, but maybe we should evade the cloud first,” Zeus said.

Athena chuckled softly. “Sending Hercules toward a storm may not be our best choice. Let’s not encourage him.”

“Aw, come on! I can lift anything!” Hercules bounced on his feet.

Suddenly, Stormy the Storm Cloud rumbled above. “Buzz off down there! I’m busy!”

Zeus frowned. “Busy creating a storm?”

“You bet!” Stormy growled. “And nobody ever thinks about how I feel.”

“We’re not here to upset you!” Zeus called. “But we need the thunderbolt you’re hiding!”

“Hiding? I’m not hiding anything!” Stormy spat, lightning crackling dramatically.

“Then why is it glowing inside you?” Athena asked, adjusting her glasses.

Stormy sighed. “It’s complicated. The thunderbolt fell into me, but I don’t know how to get it out!” 

Hercules’ eyes sparkled with determination. “I know! I can shake it out of you!”

“No!” Zeus shouted. “That will ruin everything! Just—”

But Hercules had already charged ahead. The ground shook as he stomped toward the grumpy cloud.

“Stop!” Athena yelled. “You can’t just shake Stormy!”

Stormy braced himself. “Big mistake, kid!”

Too late. Baby Hercules lifted his arms. He shook with all his strength. The very air crackled, and the earth seemed to tremble.

“Come on, thunderbolt!” Hercules shouted. “I’ll save you!”

Zeus covered his eyes. “This can’t end well…”

Stormy grumbled. “You’re driving me mad!” 

A loud BOOM echoed across the sky. Lightning flashed. A piece of the storm cloud peeled away.

“Did it come out?” Athena asked, peering. 

Hercules looked up, grinning. “I think I can feel it!”

Suddenly, a thunderous crack split the air. Out shot the thunderbolt, twinkling brightly.

But it spun wildly through the air. 

“Watch out!” Zeus cried.

Then it ricocheted towards the garden, causing flowers to explode in petals.

“Uh-oh!” Hercules barely ducked.

The thunderbolt shot toward a nearby tree. It spun. Then it stopped right above Athena.

“What now?” she said, startled. 

“Maybe it wants you!” Hercules chuckled.

But then, a second body appeared, spinning out of the storm cloud. 

“Wait!” Athena gasped. “Who—”

And just like that, everything changed.
