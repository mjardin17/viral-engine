Chapter 4: Meet Stormy the Storm Cloud

Little Zeus brushed himself off after his flight. “Who knew clouds could be so grumpy?” 

Athena looked up at him. “Stormy has been around for two hundred years. He feels ignored.”

“Ignored? But he makes rain!” Little Zeus exclaimed. “What’s the problem?” 

Athena flipped open her encyclopedia. “Stormy wants attention. He feels nobody appreciates his storms anymore.”

Baby Hercules wobbled beside them. “Me like rain! Rain fun!” His tiny fists pumped in excitement.

“Exactly,” Athena nodded. “But nobody plays in puddles anymore. Stormy wants them to notice him.”

Little Zeus scratched his head. “So, he borrowed Zeus’s thunderbolt to get their attention?”

Athena nodded. “Yes! And now we need to fix this.”

Baby Hercules helped. “Let’s climb! I can shake Stormy’s hand!”

“You’re not climbing any trees again,” Little Zeus said quickly. “The last one rejected you.”

“Hey, that tree needed a break,” Baby Hercules grinned. 

Athena waved her scroll. “We should ask him nicely. A storm cloud has feelings too.”

Little Zeus braved the winds. “Stormy! We’re here to talk!”

The massive cloud rumbled. “What do you want?” 

“We want to help!” Athena shouted. “Why did you borrow the thunderbolt?”

Stormy let out a low grumble. “Because nobody remembers me! I make rain! I do lightning! Did you even notice I’m here?” 

Little Zeus felt the weight of those words. “That’s really sad. But can we please have the thunderbolt back?”

Stormy puffed angrily, sending a bolt of lightning zipping across the sky. “You think it’s easy? No one thanks me! I’m just a forgotten cloud!”

Baby Hercules frowned. “You can come play with us!” 

“No one plays anymore!” Stormy’s voice echoed. “The people below just hurry. No one looks up.”

Athena stepped forward. “But we notice you! We appreciate you!”

Stormy paused. “Do you really mean that?”

“We do!” Little Zeus exclaimed. “We want to jump in your puddles!”

Stormy peered down, the grumpiness fading from his face. “You really want to splash with me?”

“Yes!” Baby Hercules cheered. “It’ll be fun!”

“Then why haven’t you?” Stormy asked, a hint of curiosity sparking in his voice.

Athena smiled. “It’s time to change that. Let’s make it rain!”

With a wave of her scroll, she spoke a spell. The winds howled, and raindrops began to fall.

Stormy looked shocked. “Wait, you’re really going to jump in my puddles?”

Little Zeus grinned, dodging raindrops. “Absolutely! Right after Hercules!”

Baby Hercules jumped into a puddle, splashing around with delight. He smiled up at Stormy, his expression pure joy. “See? It’s fun!”

Stormy felt warmth fill his cloud shape. “Maybe being noticed isn’t so bad after all!”

But just then, a shadow passed overhead. “What’s this?” thundered a voice, shaking the air.

A larger, darker cloud loomed next to Stormy. “You’re not the only storm cloud in town, Stormy!”

Gasps filled the air. 

Little Zeus’s eyes widened. “Who is that?”

Athena's scroll slipped from her hand. “We have to find out!”

Then a second body appeared.
