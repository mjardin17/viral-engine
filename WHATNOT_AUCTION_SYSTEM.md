## Intelligent Auction Features

### 1. **Auction Scoring & Strategy Selection**

Every product gets a confidence score (0-100) based on:
- **Category match** (collectibles, trading cards, vintage, sports = +25 to +15)
- **Condition** (mint/new/excellent = +15 to +10)
- **Price sweet spot** ($25-$200 = +10)
- **Photo quality** (3+ varied angles = +10)

**Strategy Assignment:**
- **SHOWCASE** (score 85+) → 2.8x multiplier expected, featured items
- **PREMIUM** (score 70-84) → 2.5x multiplier, collector appeal
- **VOLUME** (score 55-69) → 1.8x multiplier, steady performers
- **BULK** (score <55) → 1.3x multiplier, quick turnover

**Example:**
```
Product: Vintage Star Wars Figure
Cost: $50
Category: Collectibles (+25)
Condition: Mint (+20)
Photos: 5 angles (+10)
Price: $50 (sweet spot) (+10)
Score: 95 → SHOWCASE strategy → 2.8x expected → $140 predicted

Profit: $90 (180% ROI)
```

### 2. **Reserve Price Optimization**

- **Aggressive** (high-ROI categories): Reserve at 75% of cost → attracts bidders → drives competitive bidding
- **Balanced** (good ROI): Reserve at 80% of cost → balance safety + engagement
- **Conservative** (struggling): Reserve at 85% of cost → protect seller

Real-time adjustment based on 30+ completed auctions data.

### 3. **Batch Scheduling**

Organizes 15 items per livestream in optimal order:
1. **First 3 items** → Highest-value/rarest (viewers tune in for openers)
2. **Middle 6 items** → Volume performers (maintain engagement)
3. **Final 6 items** → Lower-confidence items (viewers exhausted, impulse buys)

**Predicted revenue per batch: $200-$500+** depending on inventory quality.

### 4. **Bid Monitoring & Fraud Prevention**

Bot 19 detects:
- **Shilling** (artificial price inflation by seller's friends)
- **Whale bidders** (high-value repeat customers — VIP list for future auctions)
- **Momentum patterns** (which item types attract competitive bidding)
