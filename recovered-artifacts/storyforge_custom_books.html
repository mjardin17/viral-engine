Questions & Answers

Will the book be approved by the prison?

Yes. We verify all books meet federal prison mail guidelines. No banned materials. No security risks. Your book arrives.

How long does delivery take?

Standard processing: 2 days. Express: 24 hours. Then shipping: 3-7 business days to the facility. Total: 5-10 days typical.

Can I include photos?

Yes! Standard: 15 photos. Premium: 30 photos. Deluxe: unlimited. All photos are automatically formatted to look professional.

What if they get transferred?

Contact us with their new facility info. We'll update the address and re-ship at no charge. We'll make sure they get it.

Can I order multiple books?

Absolutely. Different books for different occasions. Birthdays, holidays, anniversaries. Order as many as you want. 20% bulk discount at 5+.

Is this really affordable?

Yes. $29-79 for a professional book. No markup. No hidden fees. We believe everyone deserves to stay connected.

What if I don't know what to write?

We provide writing prompts and templates for each book type. Or email us—our team can help you get started for free.

Can I revise after ordering?

Standard/Premium: one free revision. Deluxe: unlimited. Contact us within 24 hours if you want to make changes.
