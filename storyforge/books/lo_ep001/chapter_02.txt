Chapter 2: The Great Search Begins

Little Zeus stood at the entrance of Hephaestus's workshop. It was loud, chaotic, and filled with smoke. He raised his voice. “Hephaestus! I need help!”

Hephaestus turned, wiping sweat from his brow. “What’s wrong?”

“I can’t find my thunderbolt!”

Hephaestus scratched his head. “Have you checked behind the sofa?”

Zeus blinked. “The sofa?”

“Sure!” Hephaestus waved his hand dismissively. “You never know.”

Zeus rushed back to his palace. He flung open the door to the throne room. He moved the sofa with all his strength. Dust flew everywhere. He ducked and peeked behind it. 

Nothing. Just three drachmas and an old sandwich.

He groaned. “Not helpful!”

Back he went to Hephaestus. “Not there! Just a sandwich!”

Hephaestus chuckled. “Well, try somewhere else. What about Poseidon?”

Zeus sighed. “I’m going to visit him now.”

With one mighty leap, he jumped to the ocean palace. Waves crashed against the rocky shore as he approached. Poseidon stood in the center of a swirling pool. He focused on the direction of the waves. Zeus shouted over the roar, “Poseidon!”

Poseidon turned, his beard flowing like seaweed. “What is it, nephew?”

“I lost my thunderbolt.”

Poseidon raised an eyebrow. “That’s troubling. Have you checked inside a whale?”

Zeus threw his hands up. “I am not checking inside a whale!”

Poseidon shrugged, a mischievous twinkle in his eye. “Suit yourself. I found mine that way.”

“I need real help!” Zeus insisted. “What if the cloud isn’t the only problem?”

Poseidon laughed. “You sure don’t like to think outside the box!”

Zeus frowned. “This isn’t a game! I need that thunderbolt!”

Poseidon considered for a moment. “Keep looking. Something will turn up, I’m sure.” 

With no more options left, Zeus dashed away from the ocean palace. He needed his friends, not some ridiculous suggestions.

His next stop? Athena. She always had a plan. 

In her study, scrolls lay piled high. Athena peered over her glasses. “What brings you here, Zeus?”

“I can’t find my thunderbolt!” He nearly shouted.

Athena placed her hand on a scroll. “Have you tried the highest mountain?”

Zeus blinked. “I haven’t checked there.”

“Then go! You might find it among the clouds!” 

Zeus nodded, excitement bubbling. “A great idea! I’ll fly there right now!”

“Don’t forget your map!” Athena called after him.

Zeus sprinted away, determined to reach the mountain. 

As he flew high, the wind whipped around him. He spotted jagged peaks in the distance. Thunder boomed. Lightning cracked ominously. 

A storm gathered above him. “Oh no!” he shouted. 

Suddenly, dark clouds twisted together. They formed into a familiar shape. “Stormy!” 

“Hey, Zeus!” the storm cloud grumbled. 

“Have you seen my thunderbolt?” Zeus pleaded.

Stormy hesitated. “Maybe. But first, I need something from you.”

“What?” 

Then, as if electrified, Stormy grumbled, “I need more lightning!” 

Zeus frowned. “You want lightning from me?”

Stormy nodded. “Yes. If you give it, I can help you find your missing treasure.”

Just then, a rumbling sound echoed. 

A shape moved in the dark clouds behind Stormy. Zeus felt a pulse of danger. 

“What’s that?” he asked nervously.

Stormy’s face turned serious. “I’m not sure what’s coming. But if it’s real trouble…” 

Then a second body appeared.
