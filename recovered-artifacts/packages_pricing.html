Frequently Asked Questions

Yes! Upgrade or downgrade anytime with prorated billing. No penalties. You pay only for what you use each month.

How long does setup take?

Typically 2-4 hours. We handle database setup, API configuration, and agent training on your specific business rules and workflows.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes! Professional package includes a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Other packages: contact sales for trial eligibility.

What's your uptime SLA?

Startup/Professional: Best-effort. Business: 99% SLA. Enterprise: 99.9% SLA with guaranteed support response times.

Do you integrate with my existing systems?

Yes. Professional: 10 integrations. Business: 25+. Enterprise: unlimited custom integrations via private API.

What about data security?

All data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS). SOC 2 Type II certified. Enterprise: private cloud deployment available.

Can I add users or seats?

Startup: 1 agent. Professional: 3 agents. Business/Enterprise: unlimited agents. Additional seats billed separately.

Do you have volume discounts?

Yes! Enterprise plans: negotiate custom pricing based on scale, integration complexity, and annual commitment.

What if I go over limits?

Startup/Professional: $0.50 per overage call, $0.10 per overage email. Business/Enterprise: no overage charges — unlimited included.
